Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Michael Jackson Discord Bot ("the Bot") processes and how it is handled.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No Voice Recording: Although the Bot joins voice channels to play audio, it does not record, stream, or store any user voice data or conversations. Audio transmission is strictly one-way (from the Bot to the channel).
  • No Permanent Storage: We do not host databases containing your personal information, user profiles, or message histories.

2. Data We Process (Temporarily)

To perform its functions, the Bot briefly processes the following Discord gateway data in real-time:

  • Command Context: User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs are processed temporarily to know where to join and execute audio playbacks.
  • Message Content: Text messages starting with the Bot's prefix are read to execute commands. Non-command messages are completely ignored.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because we do not store your data, we do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties. All data interactions happen within the official Discord API environment.

4. Data Retention

All data processed by the Bot is kept in temporary memory (RAM) only for the duration of the command execution and is wiped when the Bot restarts or the session ends.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Bot's behavior, please reach out to the bot operator directly within Discord.
